The public track record

ARGUS is a systematic equity research tool. It detects setups, scores them, tracks what actually happens to them, and publishes the result — the wins and the losses both — once a named human has reviewed and approved the figures for release.

This page has no login and shows everyone the same numbers. There is no version of this page that looks better to a prospective customer than to a skeptic. That is deliberate: a system whose entire pitch is "trust the evidence" has to let anyone check the evidence.

This is not investment advice. Nothing on this page is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security. Past performance, however honestly reported, does not predict future results.
